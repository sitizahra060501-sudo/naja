Narrative Text

Definition

A narrative text tells a story about people, animals or things, usually in the past. It has characters, a setting, a problem and a solution. Examples: fables, legends and fairy tales.

Social Function

To entertain readers and to share moral values and life lessons.

What is a fractured story?

A fractured story is a well-known tale that is retold with a twist: a new character, a new setting, a new problem or a new ending.

Original: the hare falls asleep and loses the race. Fractured: the hare and the tortoise run as a team.

Structure and Language

Past Tense

The hare slept. The tortoise won.

Conditional Tense

If the hare had not slept, he would have won.

Adjectives

Describe nouns.

proud hare, slow tortoise

Adverbs

Describe verbs.

ran quickly, walked patiently
